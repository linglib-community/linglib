How a mark is computed

  1. Fact. A judgment as printed, with its table and page: Data.Experiments.
  2. Parse. How the theory reads the experiment's sentences, by a rule on the representation the domain's theories share, or stipulated sentence by sentence at a counted cost: Eval.Parser.
  3. Prediction. The outcome the theory predicts in a cell by the experiment's linking hypothesis, or none where it says nothing: Eval.Experiment.predicted.
  4. Verdict. The prediction against the observation: Eval.Verdict.
