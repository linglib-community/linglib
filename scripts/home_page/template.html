A Lean 4 library for formal linguistics across semantics, syntax, pragmatics, morphology, phonology, and processing.

Generalization
A statement about data, which mentions no framework.
Account
A framework's construction, in the framework's own vocabulary.
Linking map
The map from an account's objects to a description of data.

What an account predicts is the range of its linking map, and theories are compared by comparing ranges.
